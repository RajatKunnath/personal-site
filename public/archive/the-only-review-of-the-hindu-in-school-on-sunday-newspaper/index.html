The Only Review Of The Hindu In School On Sunday Newspaper!

When I was planning to subscribe The Hindu In School On Sunday newspaper, the first thing I did was, I went ahead online and searched for some reviews. I was really disappointed to know that there were no reviews online about the newspaper, but not anymore. This is the full comprehensive review of The Hindu In School On Sunday newspaper.

Source - The Hindu

What is The Hindu In School On Sunday newspaper

The Hindu In School On Sunday is a 32 page weekly newspaper by The Hindu publication group. It is a newspaper that is entirely focused on kids. It covers current affairs, science, stories, literature, games, facts , sports ,etc. The newspaper comes In 2 subscription options :-

  • Annual Subscription (52 issues for 12 months) -₹1299
  • Half Yearly (26 issues for 6 months) -₹650

My Review!

The Hindu In School On Sunday is an information bank. I really don’t think that there is any thing that’s better than this. There are a lot of interesting articles on different topics on the newspaper, all of them are short, simple and are written in a way in which anyone can easily understand. The most amazing thing about The Hindu In School On Sunday Newspaper is the fact that it helps you stay up to date on important current affairs.

Source - The Hindu

The articles are less boring and are very short, crisp and precise. The content inside of the newspaper is always to the point. There is a page dedicated to sports related content mainly analytics which I really enjoy. Not only that, the literature section is very handy. Something that helps students like myself is the fact that they explain the important events and news very well, For Example :-

When the EIA Draft was announced, there was an article in The Hindu In School On Sunday, titled -

“What is EIA Draft?

Why are Environmentalists unhappy?”

On the EIA Draft article they broke down the entire news into a very simple and easy to understand format.

They also provide nice quizzes, riddles and puzzles which makes the newspaper even better. The Hindu In School on Sunday has a very good high quality paper and ink, not at all like the ones you have in a normal newspaper. The paper is more thicker and the ink is of very high quality and there is no chance of fading or anything like that. It has a little glossy texture to it. I’ve been subscribed to The Hindu In School On Sunday Newspaper for the past 5 years and those newspapers still remains clean and intact, they’ve turned a little bit yellow though.

It is also not that distracting with those advertisements like the ones you have on regular newspapers. I am not meaning to say that there are no advertisements, there are advertisements, but not many of them. The Hindu In School On Sunday is a must have for almost everyone, not only students I think it is an awesome newspaper for anyone of any age. The fact that all this information reaches your doorstep every week in 12 months for ₹1299 is stupendously awesome. Should do you buy it? Yes, You Must Buy It!
