Kindle Paperwhite(10th gen) : A Portable Library!

In this article I am going to be reviewing the Kindle Paperwhite 10th generation. Read More To Find Out.

Kindle Paperwhite. Credit-LifeWire

What is Kindle Paperwhite?

Kindle Paperwhite is an e-reader from amazon. It is one of the best in the market. It has a 6” display. Kindle Paperwhite features a display technology with E Ink Carta and Built-in-light. It also features a 300 ppi display with optimized font technology and a 16-level gray scale. The kindle Paperwhite is also waterproof. It has one of the best batteries ever in a tech product. The base model with 8gb ROM and WiFi would run you around ₹13,000 or $180.

My Review!

The kindle paperwhite is an amazing device. It is one of the best portable devices I’ve ever used. It’s just so versatile in all ways eg- The battery, Display and the selection of books available. Not to forget the fact that, how thin and light weight it is. The kindle paperwhite is just like the original kindle.

It’s just water resistant and has a more brighter display. Something else I enjoy on kindle is the highlights and notes feature. It’s just so helpful. It also has this feature wherein you can get the meaning of a word by just selecting it by long-pressing. Now, this is a killer feature for me, I use it all the time.

Another awesome thing about owning a kindle is the fact that you get books for really cheap prices although I must admit, some of the book are just about the same price of that of it’s physical copy.

Another addition to kindle is that by reading books on kindle you are actually saving the environment, ie. paper is no more required for making a book. Overall, I really like my kindle and I would encourage you to buy one if you haven’t already.

Who is a Kindle meant for?

Credit-Wikimedia Commons

Kindle is a very versatile device as I mentioned above. It is for anyone, no matter the age. You have books on kindle for all genres. Books from Comics to Novels. You can even buy your textbooks on kindle. To conclude, kindle is for everyone.

Kindle Paperwhite VS Books

When I was planning to buy the kindle my first question was, whether the kindle is good for your eyes. After a little bit of research I learnt that reading on kindle is the same as reading the physical book. That was my only problem and now that’s also solved. I love the kindle, it’s better in all ways than a physical book.

But for me, there is something about physical books that are quite special. The smell while reading the book, the nice page texture and the bright colorful cover page. I always like having a bookshelf behind me filled with good looking books, but in kindle that’s not the case.

All of these are features that only a physical book has. To be honest with you, I think that both of them has it’s own purpose and I can’t just say that the book is better or the kindle is better. Overall, it’s good to have both of them.
