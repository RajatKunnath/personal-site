## My Review

Just like the name suggests, Essentialism is a truly essential book. It was a pleasurable experience reading this book. It has also got a really good flow to it and I never experienced any lag. The concept of Essentialism is pretty self explanatory, it just means to be extremely selective about the things that you are going to do and focusing on LESS BUT BETTER.

One of my favorite quotes from this book was - "If you do not prioritize your life, then someone else will". One thing that Greg McKeown focuses quite a lot in this book was the power of saying 'NO'. Essentialism is a simple book and I am giving it a 5 star rating.

- “Instead of asking, “What do I have to give up?” essentialists ask, “What do I want to go big on?” The cumulative impact of this small change in thinking can be profound.”
