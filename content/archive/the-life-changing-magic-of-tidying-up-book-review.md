## My Review

The Life‑Changing Magic of Tidying Up is a life changing book. I must admit, that I always considered myself as a clean person but it was only after reading this book that I found out that I was really messy.

After reading the book I started enjoying the process of cleaning. I also started being more indulged in things like folding clothes, dusting etc. In the book Marie Kondo writes about the benefits of tidying, methods to tidy and tips to fold your clothes properly etcetera. I've always been so bad at folding clothes but now that has changed.

The Konmari method is very simple yet effective. "Cleaning is all about keeping what sparks joy for you and discarding the things that doesn't" - This is basically the Konmari method in a sentence. Marie Kondo also says that cleaning has the same affect as meditating under a calm waterfall.

So yeah, this is a truly life changing book and

I HIGHLY HIGHLY recommend that you read it. I am giving this wonderful book a 5 star rating.

## Notes

- **The Konmari Method**

The Konmari method is a world famous method of cleaning invented by the Japanese Consultant Marie Kondo. Konmari is basically the combination of letters in Marie Kondo's name (**Kon**do **Mari**e). Discarding is the main objective of the Konmari method. Marie Kondo also advises that we must tidy by categories rather than by rooms. Here are the 6 rules to get started with Konmari Method.

1. Commit yourself to tidying up.
2. Imagine your ideal lifestyle.
3. Discard items and thank them for serving you.
4. Tidy by category, not location.
5. Follow the right order.
6. Ask yourself it it sparks joy.

- **Tidying happens only once and it is a special moment**
- **Messy Room = Messy Mind**
- '**Some Day' never comes**
- **Remove all the badges from products that you use**
- **Empty your bag everyday**
- **Express Gratitude to things that you used that day**
