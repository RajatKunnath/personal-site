## My Review!

## 

Lone fox dancing is an impressive auto-biography written by Ruskin Bond. It is mainly about the author’s life in India as a boy and an author. He also writes about his life in Britain and why he chose India over the UK. "People are too polite there, in Britain" he says, "There is no freedom of touching each other (whether purposefully or not) like we do in India".

Something that I felt interesting when I read this book was the fact that Ruskin Bond provided some pictures of himself when he was young, he also shared pictures of some of the prominent characters in his books, like Somi, Ranbir, Kishen and most importantly his beloved father, there were pictures of others too.

![Ruskin Bond's Father.](/archive/img/c15a24_ff629039b139415f95635bb0bca6ee9f-mv2.jpg)
*Ruskin Bond's Father*

The book also had some poems which were written by Ruskin Bond about the beautiful things he saw in his daily life. The way Ruskin Bond presents things in his books always amuses me and I never get bored of his writing style. Even though this book was quite big, it was really good! So, I am giving it a 5 star rating!

## How I Discovered it?
