**Have you ever wondered how the display of a Kindle works? Kindle Displays are called as E-Ink displays. Read this article to learn more about e-ink displays.**

## What is E-Ink

E-Ink displays are basically displays that require very little power to run. It very much resembles a normal paper. The good thing about e-ink is the fact that it is good for your eyes whereas normal displays like LCD and AMOLED are not good since they emit blue light. E-Ink displays mostly comes in grayscale but today it even comes in color.

## How are they made?

The research for E-Ink displays started in an MIT lab and it was patented on 1996. The rights to the technology is currently in the hands of E-Ink corporation which was acquired by a Taiwanese company name 'Prime View International' in 2009.

![E-Ink Logo](/archive/img/c15a24_1771c121d09b45b2b8f51d507f48bc29-mv2.jpg)

E-Ink is made into thin film and then fixed into electronic devices like watches, phones, e-readers etc. Today E-Ink corporation has partnered with famous companies like Amazon, Sony, Motorola, Rakuten (Kobo reader), etcetera. There are various types of e-ink displays. The most commonly used one is called as 'E Ink Carta HD', which is used by most of the kindles.

## How does E-Ink displays work?

In e-ink technology tiny microcapsules are put in a liquid which is covered with a film layer. The microcapsules contain positively charged white particles and negatively charged black particles. First, a negative electrical field is applied and this causes the white particles to rise to the surface. In the same way a positive electrical field is applied and the black particle rises up to the surface. By applying different fields at different parts of the screen, e-ink produces a  monochromatic text display.

## Disadvantages of E-Ink Displays

1. E-Ink displays are not backlit, making it difficult to read in a dimly lit place
2. They cannot display video

## Advantages

1. It looks and feels like paper
2. It consumes less energy
3. It is good for on our eyes since it does not emit blue light.
