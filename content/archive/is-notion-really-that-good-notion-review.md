> **What exactly is** [**Notion**](notion.so)

[Notion](notion.so) is a note taking app. The thing that makes [Notion](notion.so) so awesome is the fact that you can customize it till your heart's content. Everyone's [Notion](notion.so) would be different. You can create your own template and you can share it with others, you can also collaborate and work as a team in [Notion](notion.so). I personally use [Notion](notion.so) to plan and write these articles, I sometimes use it to take notes. [Notion](notion.so) is a subscription based software.

> **How I got into** [**Notion**](notion.so) **?**

I got introduced to Notion by [Ali Abdaal](https://www.youtube.com/c/aliabdaal) in one of his videos. From then on I was looking for a good Notion Tutorial, which sadly I couldn’t find. One day I saw a course by [Francesco on Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/Your-Notion-Beginners-Guide-Quick-Start/1271045781) and I must say, that was most probably the best course I've ever had about [Notion](notion.so). Now that I know [Notion](notion.so) I started tinkering around with it and to my surprise, it worked like a charm for me. I was much more productive. I must admit the fact that it took me a little bit of time to set things up but now everything works smoothly.

> **My Review**

**First off, I love** [**Notion**](notion.so) **so this review might be a bit biased.**

[Notion](notion.so) is simple, it’s actually a mark down editor cum note taker cum whatever you want it to be. [Notion](notion.so) offers the same features you’ll get with a markdown editor, but with more advanced features. It is used to create big and huge databases and to take notes. [Notion](notion.so) is awesome because it lets you create what ever you want, how ever you want it to look like. I can even create my website inside of [Notion](notion.so).

It was a bit difficult for me to get my hands around Notion at first, but after watching that  [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/Your-Notion-Beginners-Guide-Quick-Start/1271045781) class, it was a piece of cake for me. Something I love about Notion is it’s export options, you can export your notion page as markdown, HTML and PDF. As of now my experience with [notion](notion.so) has been phenomenal. The only problem I am facing is in copying and pasting from and to [notion](notion.so), when I try to copy a paragraph with a heading, it just doesn’t work which sometimes is quite frustrating. Copying and pasting is the only thing I hate in [Notion](notion.so), otherwise I would say that [Notion](notion.so) is simply the best app ever made (at least for me).

> **BONUS My** [**Notion**](notion.so) **Setup**

If you are just starting out on [Notion](notion.so) a tip I would give you is to actually use emojis and icons a lot (if you can’t find an emoji for that particular topic you can always import any transparent png file and then it’ll look like an emoji) even if you hate emojis (just like me), trust me it’ll make you more efficient in using [Notion](notion.so), if you don’t, then it’ll look like a normal and boring text editor.

I have a very big [Notion](notion.so) setup in which there is only a single page that I constantly use named 'Blog'. Inside of  ‘Blog ’ there is a table with 4 rows tiltled- Articles  ,  Tags  ,  Done (Checkbox)  ,  Uploaded (Checkbox) . When I click on add a new section it creates a page and then I fill up the heading, tags and the date, then I continue as a normal page and start writing the content. Another thing I made was a calendar view, which makes things even easier. Overall I have a very minimalistic and easy setup since I didn’t want to overly complicate it.
