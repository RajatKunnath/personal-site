## My Notes

## 

### Highlight

A Highlight is basically **one** thing that you decide to do in a day. It should be the main task for the day. Believe in your Highlight: it is worth prioritizing over random disruption.”

1. Write your highlight down in a piece of paper
2. Do yesterday again
3. Schedule time for your highlight
4. Just say NO when someone interferes during your highlight time
5. Be a morning person

### Laser

When you are in laser mode, you are focused on your work and your mind is as focused as a laser beam. Focus on you highlight and you'll get into laser mode.

1. Make a distraction free phone (delete all distracting apps)
2. Log out of all apps after use
3. Turn off all the notifications
4. Wear a wristwatch and use it to check time rather than your phone
5. When stuck don't give up
6. The more the friction (to use a software), the less the distraction will be

### Energize

You must energize before going to Laser Mode.

1. Exercise
2. Eat real food (nuts, fruits etc)
3. Optimize coffee
4. Meditate
5. Take a 15 min short nap
6. Go outside
7. Eat without screens
8. Take out all the electronic devices from your bed room

### Reflect

Write down some notes after you finished your highlight. Learn and improve from these notes.

## How the Book Changed Me

- I've started using my daily highlights and the other make time techniques.
- I've started taking a 15 minute nap
- I've started taking TIME more seriously
