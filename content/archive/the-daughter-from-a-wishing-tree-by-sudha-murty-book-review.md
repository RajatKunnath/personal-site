> My Review

Sudha Murty's book, The Daughter From A Wishing Tree. It is a book that has tales of brave women from the Indian mythology. Honestly, the book is awesome! This book is a collection of stories, some of which I've heard and some of which I haven't.

The stories are from the ancient hindu mythology and has women as the central characters. Something that I've noticed was that usually the mythological series by Sudha Murty had some more pages but on this one it was a bit less.

![](/archive/img/c15a24_a6fcc7431cd54d3ab7f33a526667ff8e-mv2.jpg)

Sudha Murty has an explanation for that, she says that the stories about women were not written much in the ancient days, so the number of pages were less.

But the stories that are there in the book are awesome. They are all well written and explained by Sudha Murty. The book is written in the simplest form of english. Anyone of any age can easily read and understand the stories.

The descriptions are mediocre, not too good nor bad. In between the stories you can find some beautiful illustrations, making the book look even prettier. Most important thing is that this book is a great tool for women empowerment, it shows what all a woman can do.

I really enjoyed reading this book even though I  wish it had more content. Overall I would give this book a 5 star rating.

> Physical Book Specifications

The book has 180 pages, a good paper and print quality. The cover looks stunning. The best part is that  all of the copies ever printed are signed, printed signature but still good. Overall good quality and I would give it a 4 star rating.
