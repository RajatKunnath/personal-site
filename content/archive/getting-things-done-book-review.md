> What is the book 'Getting things done' about?

Getting things done is a small book by David Allen in which he talks about the basic principles of stress free productivity, systems for productivity and many more. This is a fairly small book with just 354 pages. The author of the book is a big productivity guru himself and also a professional productivity consultant. He is also a New York Times bestselling author.

> My Review

Getting things Done By David Allen is a nice book. I learnt quite a lot from this book, mainly the systems for productivity. I must admit that the first part was kind of boring, since I already had a GTD(Fancy term for ‘Getting Things Done’) system in [Notion](https://www.rajatsblog.com/post/is-notion-really-that-good-notion-review). But after the first part everything started getting better. The methods and systems explained in this book really works. Overall a pretty useful book.

> How I discovered it?

I discovered Getting Things Done about 2 years ago. It was there everywhere, everyone was talking about it, so I decided to try it out myself. I read it as an Audiobook since it’s more faster and it’s also more versatile.

> Who should read it?

I think that this book is for anyone who has some job or work. In my case, it was quite helpful since I write articles here, even though it is not technically my job. If you are a student and you are looking for how to study more productively, then, this book is not for you. I would suggest that you read the book titled ‘Make It Stick’. > My Notes

This book is filled with information so, this list isn’t really complete. These are just some of the things that I found useful from the book.

### The 5 steps of mastering workflow-

1. Capture - Collect important things.
2. Clarify - Process what it means
3. Organize - Place it in the appropriate section of your to-do list.
4. Reflect - Review that frequently.
5. Engage - Do it!

- Quantity over Quality.
- Always brainstorm projects we enter into.
- Create a system, that is easy, quick and one that works for you.
- Mind Like Water.
- Everything must be written down somewhere and keep everything out of your head.
- Ease of capturing tasks is important.
- Always be prepared for the worst things.

> How this book changed me.

I’ve noticed 3 major differences in my life after reading this book.

1. I've started being more organized.
2. I've started being more productive.
3. I also improved my existing system for capturing tasks.

Overall, Getting things done is a  pretty good book and I would give it a 4 star rating out of 5. That’s it for now,
