### Technology, comes and goes. Sometimes it’s for the good and sometimes or most of the times it causes harm rather than good. But this Man from Peru named Marino Morikawa, did the opposite.

### 

> **Who is Marino Morikawa?**

### Marino Morikawa is a 44 year old scientist from Peru. He has a master's degree in Interdisplinary Biodiplomacy and a doctorate in Environmental Sciences from the prestigious Tsukuba University in Japan.

> **Why is he popular?**

There’s a huge lake in Peru called ‘El Casajo’ . It was Marino Morikawa’s Childhood lake. The one in which he has spent most of his childhood in, playing with friends, swimming etcetera. When he grew up older he went to japan and was studying in a university.

After his university he got a job and returned home. When he returned he was disappointed to see his childhood lake highly contaminated. It was so contaminated to the point where the water became too toxic that there were no creatures in it. So what he did was mind blowing.

![](/archive/img/c15a24_469fa5cccb074cdcaf5d5f5cdf2aa0ea-mv2.jpg)

Source- T T T GRUPO MORIKAWA/Facebook

He quit his job, went to his home and started inventing a technology that would make make all the bad materials like bacteria, oils, fungus etcetera. He made a Nano bubble system that would attract all the bad particles of the lake and all the particles will be floating in the lake so that he can collect all the materials using a simple strainer and save the lake.

He was very successful at that. He created a powder which is 100% natural and harmless to the point where we humans can consume it and he named it ‘Bubble Nano Technology’. He admits that the technology he created was a bit expensive . He took a loan and started working for the lake.

![](/archive/img/c15a24_aca10c34a8ae4e7d82aafae9a655d00a-mv2.jpg)

Source- T T T GRUPO MORIKAWA/Facebook

It took him 6 months to come up with the solution and 4 months to clean the entire lake. That was a sneak peak at Marino story. Today he runs a company called Nanoplus 7 which aims to decontaminate all the water bodies in this world. Right now he is working on the decontamination of river Chira, lake Titicaca, Huacachina and Alay in Peru. He has appeared on a NatGeo Article and a Nas Daily Video. This is the man who used technology to undo the harm caused by technology.
