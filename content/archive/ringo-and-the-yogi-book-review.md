> My Review

I first saw this book on amazon as an advertisement, the cover looked really pretty so I bought it. Honestly, it was a very good book. It had a very nice story line, everything was going pretty well, the only problem was the lack of descriptions since I am very mush used to Ruskin Bond like descriptions.

The descriptions were average, not too good nor too bad. I really loved the storyline and it was keeping me hooked onto the book, the story was amazing, the addition of a character like a yogi was amazing.

I really appreciate the way Roy has described and framed Shambu the yogi. I felt as if there was some life in this book because everything in it sounded natural and that is some thing that I really enjoy and appreciate. On top of all that this book gave me some goosebumps.

The twists and turns just makes this book a must read for any child although I am not quite sure if adults would enjoy it. When I finished reading the book I really felt that this book should be made as an animated movie, it was that good! Except the descriptions, I really want to completely visualize the entire book. I am not meaning to say that the descriptions were bad, they were good but not up to the point.

I personally enjoyed reading the book, so I would give it 4.5 stars. The descriptions needs to be improve just a little bit. I really appreciate the work and effort put into the book by Prasun Roy and his team.

> Quality and Description

The book is of 184 pages. It normally goes for ₹200 in India or 3$. The quality of the page was good. The print was also very good, it was sharp and crystal clear. The only problem I had was the fact that the font size was a bit small, they could have made it a bit more bigger. The book itself was not that thick. It has a very beautiful and attractive front cover. Overall this book is of good quality.
